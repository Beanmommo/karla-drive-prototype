Mode: built-in image_gen, generated from a character reference and refined.

## Initial prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: square learner profile avatar for a cute educational mobile app.
Input image: the existing koala-in-car logo is a character reference. Create a new head-only avatar of this same koala character.
Primary request: A cute minimal hand-drawn doodle of ONLY the koala head and face. Keep the reference character's soft grey rounded head, simple vertically oval dark charcoal koala nose, tiny dot-like eyes, and completely neutral mouthless expression. Make the head large and rounded, with relatively small round ears and simple pale inner-ear circles. Complete the lower head contour as a smooth rounded chin.
Scene/backdrop: one simple solid light blue circular avatar disk, centered on a square canvas. Genuine transparent alpha outside the circle.
Composition: straight-on face, perfectly centered and balanced. Head including ears fills most of the circle, roughly 85% of its width, with a small clean margin. Entire head and ears visible, no clipping.
Style: cute educational app avatar, minimal doodle illustration, clean but slightly imperfect hand-drawn charcoal outlines, rounded shapes, flat graphic colors. Calm, friendly and approachable through proportions only.
Color palette: flat soft grey head, pale neutral inner ears, dark charcoal outlines/nose/eyes, solid light blue background.
Constraints: only a koala head. No mouth, smile, frown, eyebrows, cheek blush, expression marks or emotion. No car, wheels, body, neck, shoulders, paws, arms, accessories, text or other objects. No fur details, shading, gradients, highlights, shadows, texture, 3D effects, glow, border around the background disk, complex background or watermark. Do not copy the reference's car or any subtle lighting/texture; all color regions must be clean uniform flat fills. Output a single polished square PNG avatar.

## Final refinement prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit.
Asset type: learner profile avatar.
Edit target: the attached head-only koala avatar on a light blue circle.
Refine the illustration to match this exact minimal doodle brief. Preserve the same character, centered front-facing composition, large rounded soft grey head, tiny round charcoal eyes, simple vertical oval charcoal nose, and completely neutral MOUTHLESS face. Keep the circular light blue avatar background and transparent canvas outside the circle.
Make the ears appreciably smaller: each outer ear diameter should be about 30% of the main head width, still round and attached near the upper corners, with one simple pale inner-ear circle. Keep the large head as the dominant element filling most of the icon.

Remove ALL shading, gradients, lighting, texture and color mottling. Keep outlines slightly imperfect and hand-drawn but clean, smooth and solid; use rounded contours and minimal detail.

No mouth, smile, frown, eyebrows, blush, body, paws, neck, shoulders, car, accessories, other objects, lettering, watermark, fur lines, shadow or 3D. Output a single square PNG avatar.
